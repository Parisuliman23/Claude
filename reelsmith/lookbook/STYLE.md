# My editing style

Claude reads this before every edit and adds to it as it learns what you like.
Edit it yourself any time — plain sentences are fine.

## Brand
- Colors: (e.g. #5B4BDB violet, #FFE14D yellow)
- Fonts I like: Montserrat Black for hooks, Playfair italic for emphasis

## Captions
- Default style: bold-pop
- Highlight numbers and money amounts

## Hooks
- Short, punchy, 8 words max, all caps

## Pacing
- Cut every pause and every "um"
- Keep it under 60 seconds when possible

## Things I never want
- (e.g. emojis over my kids' faces — always use blur)
