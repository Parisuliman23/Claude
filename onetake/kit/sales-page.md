## THE PROBLEM
You love making content. You don't love the 2 hours in CapCut after every 60-second video:
cutting every "um", syncing captions, hunting for B-roll that actually matches what you said, timing every pop-up by
hand, and then doing it all again tomorrow.

Hiring an editor costs hundreds a month, and they still don't edit like you.

## THE SOLUTION
**One Take is a reel editing engine that runs on your own computer with Claude.**
Drop your clips in a folder, say "I have a new reel to edit", and Claude does the rough cut and the first styling pass.
Then it opens your studio: you see everything live, change anything, and nothing renders until you press
**Lock in my looks**.

## HOW IT WORKS
1. **Film like normal.** Retakes are fine. One Take keeps your best take of every line.
2. **Drop your clips in.** Claude cuts the pauses, filler words and false starts, fixes misheard words in the captions,
   and styles it: a hook, captions, graphics on the exact word you say them, B-roll that shows what you're talking
   about, music and sound effects.
3. **Review in your studio.** Bring back any cut, swap any graphic, type changes to Claude ("make this number count
   up"), then lock it in and download your MP4, or send it to CapCut with every layer editable.
