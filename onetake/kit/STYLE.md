## Core rules (good defaults: keep, change or delete)
1. **The talking head is the spine.** Graphics orbit me; my face is never covered. Big graphics and B-roll go in a
   split screen (graphic on top, me below).
2. **Visuals land on the word.** Every noun, number and claim gets a visual on the exact word it's spoken.
3. **On-screen text paraphrases, never transcribes.** Short headlines, no full sentences.
4. **Hook in the first 1–3 seconds**: a short headline from the first frame, no warm-up.
5. **Cut tight**: start every line on its first word; cut false starts, repeated takes and long pauses. Keep the
   best take of each line, and never drop a line I only said once.
6. **B-roll must show what I'm saying**: never a random "close enough" clip. A panel with the words, a logo or a chart
   beats a vague clip.
7. **One accent colour at a time**, max 2 typefaces + captions. ~90% of text is white.
8. **Motion**: graphics snap or pop in (no slow fades); lists build item by item as I say them; numbers count up or
   pop on the word.
9. **Sound**: my voice natural and calm; soft music underneath; a whoosh on real footage cut-aways, a soft pop on
   pops and lists, a ding on the CTA. Fewer, not more.
10. **No colour filter** unless I ask: my footage should look like it does on my phone.
11. **End on my last real sentence** with a specific CTA keyword ("comment WORD"), not a generic "follow for more".

## Where things go (y = 0 top, 1 bottom)
- y 0.06–0.20: hook headline, chapter header, CTA keyword, a list above my head.
- y 0.20–0.40: screenshots, photos, charts (above my head) or the top half of a split screen.
- y 0.44–0.70: captions (never over my mouth), small pops.
- y 0.70–0.88: name badges, profile cards (keep clear of Instagram's caption and buttons; `edge-check` helps).
