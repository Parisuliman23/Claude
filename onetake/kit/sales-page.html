The problem

Two hours in CapCut for a sixty-second video.

Cutting every "um". Syncing captions. Hunting for B-roll that actually matches what you said. Timing every pop-up by hand. Then doing it all again tomorrow. An editor costs hundreds a month, and still doesn't edit like you.

How it works

Three steps. You only do two.

STEP 1

Film like normal

Retakes are fine. One Take keeps your best take of every line and never drops a line you said once.

STEP 2

Drop your clips in

Say "I have a new reel to edit". Claude cuts the pauses and retakes, fixes misheard captions, and styles it: hook, captions, graphics on the word, B-roll, music, sound.

STEP 3

Review, then lock it in

Bring back any cut, swap any graphic, ask Claude for changes. Nothing renders until you press Lock in my looks.
