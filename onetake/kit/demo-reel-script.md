# One Take: demo reel script (~60 s)

**Goal:** show the result, not how it's built. Viewers should think "I want that", then comment for the free
version / waitlist. Film yourself talking (front), plus screen recordings of the studio (phone or desktop).
Edit it WITH One Take: the reel is the proof.

## Hook options (pick one, first 2 seconds, text on screen)
1. "I haven't opened CapCut in a month." *(on screen: "my editor is Claude")*
2. "I filmed this in one take. I didn't edit it." *(show the raw clip with all the flubs, then snap to the edit)*
3. "This reel edited itself. Here's what it did."

## Beats
| Time | You say (paraphrase in your own words) | On screen |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 s | Hook (above) | You talking + the hook headline |
| 3–8 s | "Here's my raw footage: pauses, ums, I said the intro four times." | Split screen: the raw clip playing, a counter "4 takes" |
| 8–14 s | "I drop the clips in a folder and say: I have a new reel to edit." | Screen recording: dragging clips into drop-in-order, typing the sentence |
| 14–22 s | "It cuts every pause and every retake, and keeps my best take." | Studio, Cut tab: the script with cut lines in brackets, a line coming back with one tap |
| 22–32 s | "Captions, graphics on the exact word I say them, B-roll that actually shows what I'm talking about." | Before/after split, then 3 quick shots: a number popping on the word, a checklist building, a split-screen cut-away |
| 32–40 s | "And it learns my style. Every change I make, it remembers for next time." | Studio: you delete a graphic → next reel it's gone; the "Learned from my edits" note |
| 40–47 s | "Nothing renders until I hit Lock in my looks. Then it's an MP4, or a CapCut project with every layer." | Tap "Lock in my looks", the render bar, CapCut opening the project with layers |
| 47–54 s | "Carousels and trial reels from the same video, in one tap." | Finish tab: carousel slides, 3 trial reels |
| 54–60 s | CTA (below) | Clean shot of you + the CTA card |

## Shot list (film/record before editing)

## Rules for every launch reel
- Show results and features; never show the code, the folders' insides or how it's built.
- Real footage only (yours). No fake numbers: if you mention time saved, use your own real numbers.
- Leave Meg's product, name and branding out: One Take is its own thing.
