# Animation breakdown: every graphic, when it appears, and why

How this was built: each video was sampled at 1 frame per second to list every on-screen element. The 20 most important animations were then pulled frame by frame (every 2nd–4th frame at native 24/30 fps) to measure entry timing, duration and easing. Those frame strips are in [`frame-strips/`](frame-strips/). Times are `seconds` from the start of each video.

**How to read the "Why" column:** every visual answers one of five jobs:

| Job | What it does | Typical asset |
|---|---|---|
| **PROOF** | Shows that a claim is real | Screenshot, dashboard, article, chart, post |
| **NAME** | Puts a face or object to a noun the second it's said | Person photo, logo badge, thumbnail, product |
| **STRUCTURE** | Shows where we are in the argument | Title, numbered list, chapter header, checklist |
| **FEEL** | Shows the emotion behind a line | Emoji, meme GIF, reaction clip, B&W parody |
| **ACT** | Tells the viewer what to do | "Comment ___", arrow, +follow, profile card |

### Timing rules that showed up in every video
1. **Graphics land on the word, not before it.** Entry is within 0–2 frames of the noun or number being spoken.
2. **Entrances are fast; exits are hard cuts.** Almost nothing fades out slowly. Pop-ins are ≤3 frames. "Drawn" elements (arrows, boxes, gauges, highlighters) take 0.3–1.3 s and use ease-out.
3. **Lists accumulate.** Items are added, never swapped, until the section ends. Then the whole block clears at once.
4. **Exits happen on topic change.** Exits line up with a new sentence or a punch-in, not with a timer.
5. **The final 3–5 seconds are clean**: no graphics, or only the CTA.
